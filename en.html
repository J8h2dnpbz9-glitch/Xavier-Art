Artist

Xavier is a visual artist whose work emerges from curiosity, observation, and an interest in exploring new possibilities within art. Each piece results from a process in which the initial idea evolves through work with materials, textures, and composition.

He understands each work as a unique piece, developed without haste and with the conviction that the final result depends as much on the idea as on the care devoted to every stage of the process.

Through this space, he shares a selection of original works with the intention of bringing his practice closer to collectors, art lovers, and people interested in discovering pieces created with authenticity, dedication, and attention to detail.
